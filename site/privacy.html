Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Overview

CartelTV is an Android TV launcher. This policy explains the information the app handles when you use it.

Information you provide

  • Provider credentials you enter, such as IPTV portal, Xtream, Trakt, Simkl, Real-Debrid, or TorBox credentials.
  • OAuth tokens issued by services you choose to connect, including Google/YouTube.
  • Location used to provide local weather, when you grant location permission.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, authenticate to connected services, display account content, and maintain app settings. Credentials are stored locally on your device.

Third-party services

CartelTV connects to third-party services you configure, including your IPTV provider, metadata providers, weather providers, and streaming services. Their own privacy policies apply to the information they process.

Google / YouTube access

When you connect Google/YouTube, CartelTV requests read-only access to view your YouTube account content such as subscriptions, liked videos, and Watch Later. The app does not post, comment, upload, or modify your YouTube account. Tokens are stored locally and can be revoked through your Google Account permissions.

Data sharing and sale

CartelTV does not sell personal information. Information is shared with a connected service only as needed to provide the feature you requested.

Your choices

You can sign out of connected services, clear app data, revoke OAuth permissions, or uninstall the app at any time.
