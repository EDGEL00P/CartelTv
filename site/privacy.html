Privacy

A short, plain-language summary of how CartelTV handles your information.

Stored on your device

Provider credentials, app settings, and sign-in tokens are stored locally on your TV.

Services you connect

Information is sent only to the services you choose to connect, so CartelTV can provide the features you request.

Google / YouTube

YouTube access is read-only. CartelTV can view your account content but does not post, comment, upload, or modify it.

Your control

You can sign out, revoke access, clear app data, or uninstall CartelTV at any time. Personal information is not sold.
